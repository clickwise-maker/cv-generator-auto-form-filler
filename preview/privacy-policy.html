Privacy Policy

1. Summary

  • We collect as little as possible. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.
  • To open the builder from this site you sign in with email and password or with Google. Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase), which stores your email address, your name if you give it, and a hashed password.

2. Your CV data

  • The CV you type (name, contact details, experience, education, skills) is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. It is not uploaded to us while you edit.
  • Clearing your browser data, or using a different browser or device, removes or hides that saved CV. Please keep your own copy (download it).
  • When you request a clean (paid) download or start a purchase, your CV content is sent to our server only to create the file and check the order. We keep order records (order ID, plan, amount, status, payment reference and a fingerprint/hash of the CV) — not the CV text itself.

3. Auto Form Filler

When you use a form-fill credit, the company name and the result (success or failure) are recorded so that credits are counted correctly and refunded if a fill fails.

4. Payments

  • CV Studio is currently in trial mode and no real payments are taken yet.
  • When payments are switched on, they are processed by a payment provider (Razorpay). We never see or store your card or UPI details.

5. Other services we use

  • Our hosting provider (Render) receives normal technical request data such as IP address and browser type.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives a normal request from your browser.

6. Cookies and local storage

  • We use browser local/session storage to keep your CV, settings and download receipts on your device. Sign-in uses storage to keep you logged in.
  • We do not use advertising cookies.

7. Your choices

  • You can delete your saved CV at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this website.
  • To ask about, or delete, any order record or account we hold, email us.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.

9. Contact

Email / ईमेल: team@clickwisewe24in.we24.in

2. आपका CV डेटा

  • आप जो CV लिखते हैं (नाम, संपर्क, अनुभव, शिक्षा, स्किल) वह आपके अपने डिवाइस पर ब्राउज़र के local storage में सेव होता है। एडिट करते समय यह हमें अपलोड नहीं होता।

  • जब आप क्लीन (पेड) डाउनलोड माँगते हैं या खरीद शुरू करते हैं, तब आपका CV कंटेंट सिर्फ़ फ़ाइल बनाने और ऑर्डर जाँचने के लिए हमारे सर्वर पर भेजा जाता है। हम ऑर्डर रिकॉर्ड (ऑर्डर ID, प्लान, राशि, स्थिति, पेमेंट रेफ़रेंस और CV का फ़िंगरप्रिंट/हैश) रखते हैं — CV का टेक्स्ट नहीं।

3. ऑटो फ़ॉर्म फ़िलर

फ़ॉर्म-फ़िल क्रेडिट इस्तेमाल होने पर कंपनी का नाम और नतीजा (सफल/असफल) रिकॉर्ड होता है, ताकि क्रेडिट सही गिने जाएँ और फ़िल असफल होने पर वापस हों।
