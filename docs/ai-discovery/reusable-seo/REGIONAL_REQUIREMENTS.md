## EU/EEA: Poland, Spain, France, Germany and Portugal

**Primary text read:** GDPR https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng (`gdpr`), Articles 3,13,27 and recital 23. Article 3 covers EU establishments, and non-EU processing related to offering services to people in the Union or monitoring their behaviour; payment is not necessary. Recital 23 says mere website accessibility is insufficient to establish targeting. Article 13 requires controller/contact, purposes/legal basis, recipients, applicable transfer information, retention/criteria and rights. Article 27 has conditional representative requirements and exceptions—not every overseas site automatically requires one.

- [ ] Decide whether establishment/targeting/monitoring triggers apply; document relevant facts. Localized country marketing may be relevant, but a language URL alone is not a legal assessment.
- [ ] Reconcile no-account privacy wording with actual login; identify controller, lawful grounds, processors, retention and rights procedure; assess Art.27 applicability/exemptions with counsel.
- [ ] Assess each non-EEA recipient/transfer against current Chapter V mechanisms. The Art.13 transfer-information requirement was read; no current adequacy list, SCC implementation or vendor contract was verified in this task.
- [ ] Review national ePrivacy implementations, especially trackers/local storage. Directive 2002/58/EC Article 5(3), read at https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02002L0058-20091219 (`eprivacy`), sets consent/information for storage/access and exceptions for transmission or strictly necessary requested services. Do NOT convert this to “every cookie requires consent.” Inventory ads/analytics before classifying them. Country-specific exemptions and CMP-provider policy are not verified here.
