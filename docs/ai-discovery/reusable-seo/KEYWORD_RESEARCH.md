**Status: qualitative vocabulary and intent mapping; NOT completed quantified demand research.** No Keyword Planner, Search Console query export, Trends series or paid provider metrics were accessed. All volume/competition/difficulty values are null; no provider/country/time-window evidence exists. Competitor text demonstrates wording, not search frequency, ranking or native-speaker approval.

## Evidence method
B = exact phrase found in a public publisher title/body saved under evidence/. C = researcher candidate, unverified as a real query. Search-result snippets are not silently promoted to fetched page evidence. Country fields identify proposed research markets, NOT service availability. See PRIOR_CLAIMS_RECHECK.json: all 34 inherited citations were rechecked; only 5 exact phrases survived unchanged; inaccessible/mismatched claims were downgraded or replaced with actual observed wording.

## Product guardrails
Sign-in required. Free clean (watermark-free) Color PDF and Plain PDF; three templates; no numeric free quota established. Do not call it unlimited. No active generative AI endpoint established. English OCR only, input cap 12 MiB, scanned-PDF fallback two pages. Local heuristic proposals are not arbitrary-site autofill, autoapply or PDF write-back. No paid checkout availability promise. ## Market distinctions
- Portuguese: /pt/ content must choose a declared audience. pt-BR observations do not validate pt-PT. Eight Portugal variants remain candidates, with no country demand evidence. Portugal conditional GDPR review is separate from Brazil LGPD.
- Japanese: observed publishers distinguish 履歴書 and 職務経歴書. This is vocabulary evidence only; FormZalo is not verified to produce either required local format, accepted layouts, or correct Japanese PDF glyphs. Hold format-specific claims pending actual output tests and native review.
- English IN/US/GB and Arabic SA/AE are proposed separate measurement markets; a single global phrase cannot be assigned combined volume.
- The first five priority locales each have read vocabulary evidence, but form/AI intent demand remains largely unverified. The other seven also have read resume vocabulary evidence, not completed market validation.

## Content decisions
Merge free-builder and CV-maker variants into useful resume clusters. Existing resume pages can describe only tested features and sign-in requirements. AI-generation, autoapply, arbitrary web autofill and PDF-writeback phrases are research/guide opportunities, not current feature landing-page claims. Do not create duplicate pages per synonym or market. Prioritize screenshots, limitations, steps and output examples; validate each script before advertising language-specific exports.
